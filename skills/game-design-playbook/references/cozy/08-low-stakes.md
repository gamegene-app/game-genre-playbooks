# How much tension and failure belong in a relaxed game?

**Also matches:** no fail state, stress-free, relaxing, pressure, losing in a cozy game, difficulty

**What to weigh:** Tension can stay if failure never feels like the player's clumsiness or a real threat; take away the fear and keep the pressure of a decision.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes

At event demos Ben Kane saw kids bossing their parents around and players enjoying the talking itself, and concluded the team was making a communication party game that just happens to have bombs. They removed the serious bomb references to keep it lighthearted, though still tense.

- Source: [Finding the fun in bomb defusal with Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2016)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/f5008e04-b3f7-4938-bbca-416a34bf1bca

#### Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes

Steel Crate Games dropped the idea of making the defuser's part technically demanding, not wanting players to feel they lost for being bad at video games. Demos also showed that lifting off the headset to find buttons got in the way, so they left motion controls for a gamepad. Ben Kane says the decisions are the hard part, keeping the talk off blaming one player's execution.

- Source: [#98: Ben Kane on designing Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes](https://voicesofvr.com/98-ben-kane-on-designing-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes/) (Voices of VR, 2015)
- Source: [Finding the fun in bomb defusal with Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2016)

## 日本語

# くつろげるゲームに、緊張と失敗をどれだけ置くか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 失敗しない, ストレスの無い, 癒やし, 圧, 負けない, 難しさ

**何を量るか:** 失敗が遊び手の不器用さや本物の脅威に感じられなければ、緊張は残してよい。怖さは外し、判断の重さは残す。

### この判断をした作品

#### Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes

ベン・ケインは展示会の試遊で、子どもが親に指図して楽しむなど、遊び手が話すこと自体を楽しむ姿を見て、作っているのは爆弾のゲームではなく会話で遊ぶパーティーゲームだと考えた。そこで深刻な爆弾の要素を外し、緊張はあっても怖くない作りにした。

- 出典: [Finding the fun in bomb defusal with Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2016)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/f5008e04-b3f7-4938-bbca-416a34bf1bca

#### Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes

スティール・クレート・ゲームズは、爆弾を触る側の操作を技術的に難しくする案を、ゲームが下手だから負けたと感じさせたくないので採らなかった。試遊でヘッドセットを外してボタンを探すのが妨げになると分かり、動きの操作もやめてゲームパッドにした。ベン・ケインによれば、難しさは判断に置き、会話が誰かの操作を責める方へそれないようにした。

- 出典: [#98: Ben Kane on designing Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes](https://voicesofvr.com/98-ben-kane-on-designing-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes/) (Voices of VR, 2015)
- 出典: [Finding the fun in bomb defusal with Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2016)
