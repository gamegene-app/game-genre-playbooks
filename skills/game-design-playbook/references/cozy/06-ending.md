# Should a cozy game end, or go on forever?

**Also matches:** endless mode, ending, live service, content updates, post-game

**What to weigh:** An ending lets the player leave satisfied; an endless world keeps them, but can turn into a treadmill they resent.

No recorded precedent yet for this question. Ask GameGene to research it: https://gamegene.app/en/feedback?panel=submit

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Wilmot's Warehouse

Richard Hogg writes that the game takes about six hours, has a finite end by deliberate design and will never get an endless mode. He quotes Ricky Haggett: keeping the warehouse in equilibrium forever or making it harder until players could not continue were possible, but neither felt like a satisfying end, and keeping players on the hamster wheel did not feel ethical.

- Source: [Game Design Deep Dive: The creative camaraderie behind Wilmot's Warehouse](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-the-creative-camaraderie-behind-i-wilmot-s-warehouse-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2019)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/cca7dc47-8690-4dee-b51c-62de23e2b92a

### Games that went the other way

#### Club Penguin

Aiming to distract children, Club Penguin's makers chose to run it like a kids' activity magazine such as OWL rather than a game, with new content and challenges every week. Rooms repeat a loop of earning coins in a minigame, spending them in a shop and moving on to socialize, which Priebe likens to how Disneyland and towns are laid out.

- Source: [Club Penguin: An interview with co-founder Lance Priebe](https://www.studio.dirt.fyi/p/club-penguin) (Studio Dirt)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/e7f0f22a-31ba-4adb-b61c-b55a3548a84b

## 日本語

# コージーなゲームに終わりを置くか、ずっと続けさせるか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 終わりの無いモード, エンディング, 運営型, 更新, クリア後

**何を量るか:** 終わりがあると、遊び手は満ち足りて離れられる。終わらない世界は引き留めるが、恨めしい回し車になりうる。

この問いの先例はまだ記録に無い。GameGene に調査を依頼する: https://gamegene.app/feedback?panel=submit

### この判断をした作品

#### Wilmot's Warehouse

リチャード・ホッグは、本作は約6時間で終わり、終わりを持たせたのは意図した設計で、終わりの無いモードは入れないと書く。そこに引くリッキー・ハゲットの言では、倉庫を釣り合ったまま永遠に続けさせる作りも、難しくし続けて先へ進めなくする作りも採れたが、どちらも満足のいく終わりではなく、遊び手を回し車に乗せ続けるのは倫理に反するという。

- 出典: [Game Design Deep Dive: The creative camaraderie behind Wilmot's Warehouse](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-the-creative-camaraderie-behind-i-wilmot-s-warehouse-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2019)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/cca7dc47-8690-4dee-b51c-62de23e2b92a

### 逆を選んだ作品

#### Club Penguin

クラブペンギンの作り手は子どもの気をそらそうと、ゲームとしてでなく OWL のような子ども向け活動雑誌のように運営し、毎週新しい中身と挑戦を出すことにした。部屋にはミニゲームでコインを稼ぎ、店で使い、社交の場へ移る流れが繰り返し在り、プリービはディズニーランドや町の作り方と同じだと言う。

- 出典: [Club Penguin: An interview with co-founder Lance Priebe](https://www.studio.dirt.fyi/p/club-penguin) (Studio Dirt)
