# Should the player arrange the world their own way?

**Also matches:** decorating, sorting, inventory, self-expression, room design, organizing

**What to weigh:** Leaving the order to the player (how to sort, where to put things) makes the space theirs; it needs things that can be grouped in more than one way.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Wilmot's Warehouse

Richard Hogg worked in two warehouses and a picture archive when young, and enjoyed knowing the stock and optimizing his routines more for pleasure than necessity. For the game he gave 500 picture items deliberately overlapping, ambiguous connections and left it to the player to decide how to group and place them.

- Source: [Road to the IGF: Richards Hogg & Haggett's Wilmot's Warehouse](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-richards-hogg-haggett-s-i-wilmot-s-warehouse-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2018)
- Source: [Game Design Deep Dive: The creative camaraderie behind Wilmot's Warehouse](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-the-creative-camaraderie-behind-i-wilmot-s-warehouse-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2019)

## 日本語

# 遊び手に、自分なりの並べ方・飾り方をさせるか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 飾る, 整理, 持ち物, 自己表現, 部屋づくり, 片付け

**何を量るか:** 並べ方を遊び手に任せる (どう分け、どこに置くか) と、その場所は遊び手のものになる。そのぶん、何通りにも分けられる物が要る。

### この判断をした作品

#### Wilmot's Warehouse

リチャード・ホッグは若いころ2つの倉庫と写真資料館で働き、品物を覚えて手順を磨くことを、必要よりも楽しみのためにやっていた。本作では500点の絵の品にわざと重なり合う曖昧なつながりを持たせ、どう分けてどこへ置くかを遊び手に決めさせた。

- 出典: [Road to the IGF: Richards Hogg & Haggett's Wilmot's Warehouse](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-richards-hogg-haggett-s-i-wilmot-s-warehouse-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2018)
- 出典: [Game Design Deep Dive: The creative camaraderie behind Wilmot's Warehouse](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-the-creative-camaraderie-behind-i-wilmot-s-warehouse-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2019)
