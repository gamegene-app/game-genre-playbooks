# Which camera and viewpoint make it scary?

**Also matches:** first person vs third person, fixed camera, over-the-shoulder, player body, embodiment, framing

**What to weigh:** First person puts the threat on the player; a fixed or distant camera can hide what is in the room, but makes the threat aim at a character on screen.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Alien: Isolation

To get the project approved, Alistair Hope accepted a third-person camera and the game was built that way for a long time. In testing it was less scary, as if the alien were hunting the character rather than the player; after a prototype fixed the camera to the heroine's head, the team returned to first person. Hope said third person felt like an Alien game and first person felt like Alien.

- Source: [How Creative Assembly brought fear to Alien: Isolation](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-creative-assembly-brought-fear-to-i-alien-isolation-i-) (Game Developer, 2015)
- Source: [How Creative Assembly Built Its Perfectly Evolved AI Xenomorph](https://www.gameinformer.com/retrospective/2025/06/24/how-creative-assembly-built-its-perfectly-evolved-ai-xenomorph) (Game Informer, 2025)

#### Resident Evil

Resident Evil's fixed camera did not begin as a horror choice. Shinji Mikami says the game was first planned in first person, but the 3D of the time could not produce the visuals he wanted, so after seeing Alone in the Dark he switched to pre-rendered backgrounds and fixed angles. The scare of a new angle revealing what is already in the room grew out of that constraint.

- Source: [Two decades later, Mikami recounts the origin story of Resident Evil](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/two-decades-later-mikami-recounts-the-origin-story-of-i-resident-evil-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2016)
- Source: [Archipel «Shinji Mikami, Part 1: The birth of the survival horror game» (2020年10月)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKYX3GstHlw&t=723s) (Archipel)

#### Iron Lung

David Szymanski gave Iron Lung a first-person character controller that walks inside the submarine. He felt that if the player only controlled the sub, or sat in a chair steering it, the sub would become the avatar and any sense of danger to a human would be lost. Walking inside makes the cramped space and being trapped physical; the sub is not the protagonist and in some ways is also an antagonist.

- Source: [Dusk and the Art of Making Short Games with David Szymanski (Episode 1712 transcript)](https://softwareengineeringdaily.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/SED1712-Dusk.txt) (Software Engineering Daily, 2024)

## 日本語

# どのカメラと視点なら怖くなるか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 一人称, 三人称, 固定カメラ, 肩越し, 体の感覚, 画角

**何を量るか:** 一人称は脅威を遊び手自身に向ける。固定や遠いカメラは部屋の中を隠せるが、脅威が狙うのは画面の人物になる。

### この判断をした作品

#### Alien: Isolation

企画を通すため、アリステア・ホープは三人称で作ることを受け入れ、長くその形で開発した。だが試すと怖さが足りず、エイリアンが狙うのは自分でなく画面の人物だと感じられた。カメラを主人公の頭に付けた試作を見て一人称へ戻し、«三人称はエイリアンのゲーム、一人称はエイリアンそのもの» と語った。

- 出典: [How Creative Assembly brought fear to Alien: Isolation](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-creative-assembly-brought-fear-to-i-alien-isolation-i-) (Game Developer, 2015)
- 出典: [How Creative Assembly Built Its Perfectly Evolved AI Xenomorph](https://www.gameinformer.com/retrospective/2025/06/24/how-creative-assembly-built-its-perfectly-evolved-ai-xenomorph) (Game Informer, 2025)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/464e6d31-34ae-4e88-b1e1-62bd8ee89b44

#### Resident Evil

バイオハザードの固定カメラは、恐怖のための選択として始まったものではない。三上真司によれば最初は一人称で企画したが、当時の3Dでは思った画が出ず、アローン・イン・ザ・ダークを見てプリレンダの背景と固定の画角に切り替えた。画角が切り替わるたびに部屋の中のものが姿を見せる怖さは、その制約から生まれた。

- 出典: [Two decades later, Mikami recounts the origin story of Resident Evil](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/two-decades-later-mikami-recounts-the-origin-story-of-i-resident-evil-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2016)
- 出典: [Archipel «Shinji Mikami, Part 1: The birth of the survival horror game» (2020年10月)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKYX3GstHlw&t=723s) (Archipel)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/bf5cd0c7-2eaf-4740-a521-d0e07468ff65

#### Iron Lung

デヴィッド・シマンスキーは、FPS の操作キャラクターを潜水艇の中に置き、艇内を歩かせた。艇だけを操る作りや椅子に座って動かす作りでは、操る艇が遊び手の分身になり、人の身に危険が迫る感覚が消えると考えたからだ。歩ける体があることで狭さと閉じ込められた感じが体に届く。艇は主人公ではなく、ある意味では敵でもある。
