# How should fear be paced so players keep going?

**Also matches:** pacing, tension and release, too scary to finish, jump scares, breathing room, intensity curve

**What to weigh:** Constant fear numbs or exhausts; starting gentle, spacing scares and letting the threat withdraw keep the player able to continue.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly

The first Fatal Frame was called too scary to sell, and many players said they were too frightened to finish it. For Crimson Butterfly, Makoto Shibata moved to a dreamlike mood that starts gently and grows more frightening, and leaned on the story to carry players through to the end.

- Source: [零～刺青の聲～ コラム 第1夜](http://www.tecmo.co.jp/product/zero3/z60c01.htm) (テクモ)

## 日本語

# 遊び手が投げ出さないよう、怖さの波をどう配るか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 緩急, 怖すぎて進めない, 驚かし, 息抜き, 緊張と緩和, テンポ

**何を量るか:** ずっと怖いと慣れるか疲れ切る。穏やかに始め、驚かしの間を空け、脅威を退かせることで、遊び手は先へ進める。

### この判断をした作品

#### Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly

初代『零』は «怖すぎて売れない» と言われ、怖くて最後まで遊べないという声も多かった。柴田誠は『紅い蝶』を幻想的な雰囲気にし、最初は怖くなく、だんだん怖くなる流れと物語の引きで遊び手を最後まで連れていく作りに変えた。
