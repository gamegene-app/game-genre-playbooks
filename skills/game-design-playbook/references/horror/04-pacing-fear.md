# How should fear be paced so players keep going?

**Also matches:** pacing, tension and release, too scary to finish, jump scares, breathing room, intensity curve, AI director, safe mode

**What to weigh:** Constant fear numbs or exhausts; starting gentle, spacing scares and letting the threat withdraw keep the player able to continue.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Left 4 Dead

Mike Booth avoided hand-placed scripts because players learn them quickly, and let the AI Director populate threats. Separately, to pace the game it estimates each survivor's intensity and holds back major threats while it runs high; boss encounters are exempt from this.

- Source: [Replayable Cooperative Game Design: Left 4 Dead (GDC 2009 slides)](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/apps/valve/2009/GDC2009_ReplayableCooperativeGameDesign_Left4Dead.pdf) (Valve, 2009)
- Source: [The Making Of... Left 4 Dead](https://www.gamesradar.com/making-left-4-dead/) (GamesRadar+ (Edge), 2015)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/6edb4751-ce2d-42a9-a6fe-cb1c7c1b3e7c

#### Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly

The first Fatal Frame was called too scary to sell, and many players said they were too frightened to finish it. For Crimson Butterfly, Makoto Shibata moved to a dreamlike mood that starts gently and grows more frightening, and leaned on the story to carry players through to the end.

- Source: [零～刺青の聲～ コラム 第1夜](http://www.tecmo.co.jp/product/zero3/z60c01.htm) (テクモ)
- Source: [Fatal Frame 2 Hits PSN Tuesday, Series Director Speaks](https://blog.playstation.com/2013/05/02/fatal-frame-2-hits-psn-tuesday-series-director-speaks/) (PlayStation.Blog, 2013)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/2cb1ad43-8a72-4321-830c-5e814a93b8a1

#### SOMA

Frictional considered a no-death mode before release but skipped it, Thomas Grip says, to deliver a certain experience and a clear message. Players later found the monsters too stressful and the fan mod Wuss Mode made them harmless. In 2017 Frictional added Safe Mode: you cannot die, but monsters may still be dangerous if you push your luck, keeping the world inhospitable.

- Source: [Frictional on designing SOMA's new monster-free Safe Mode](https://www.pcgamer.com/frictional-on-designing-somas-new-monster-free-safe-mode/) (PC Gamer, 2017)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/5ccf670d-7102-4827-b137-8426a5379c9e

## 日本語

# 遊び手が投げ出さないよう、怖さの波をどう配るか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 緩急, 怖すぎて進めない, 驚かし, 息抜き, 緊張と緩和, テンポ, 監督役, 死なないモード

**何を量るか:** ずっと怖いと慣れるか疲れ切る。穏やかに始め、驚かしの間を空け、脅威を退かせることで、遊び手は先へ進める。

### この判断をした作品

#### Left 4 Dead

マイク・ブースは、手で置いた台本は遊び手にすぐ覚えられるとして避け、脅威の配置を AI ディレクターに任せた。緩急は別の仕組みで、生存者ごとの高ぶりを見積もり、高すぎれば大きな脅威をしばらく引っ込める。ボス戦はこの調整の外に置いた。

- 出典: [Replayable Cooperative Game Design: Left 4 Dead (GDC 2009 slides)](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/apps/valve/2009/GDC2009_ReplayableCooperativeGameDesign_Left4Dead.pdf) (Valve, 2009)
- 出典: [The Making Of... Left 4 Dead](https://www.gamesradar.com/making-left-4-dead/) (GamesRadar+ (Edge), 2015)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/6edb4751-ce2d-42a9-a6fe-cb1c7c1b3e7c

#### Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly

初代『零』は «怖すぎて売れない» と言われ、怖くて最後まで遊べないという声も多かった。柴田誠は『紅い蝶』を幻想的な雰囲気にし、最初は怖くなく、だんだん怖くなる流れと物語の引きで遊び手を最後まで連れていく作りに変えた。

- 出典: [零～刺青の聲～ コラム 第1夜](http://www.tecmo.co.jp/product/zero3/z60c01.htm) (テクモ)
- 出典: [Fatal Frame 2 Hits PSN Tuesday, Series Director Speaks](https://blog.playstation.com/2013/05/02/fatal-frame-2-hits-psn-tuesday-series-director-speaks/) (PlayStation.Blog, 2013)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/2cb1ad43-8a72-4321-830c-5e814a93b8a1

#### SOMA

フリクショナル・ゲームズは発売前に死なないモードを考えたが、決まった体験とはっきりした伝え方のため外したとトーマス・グリップは語る。発売後、怪物がつらいという声が届き、ファンの改造 «Wuss Mode» が怪物を無害にした。2017年に «セーフモード» を足し、死なないが運を試しすぎれば怪物は危険なままにして、住みにくい世界を保った。

- 出典: [Frictional on designing SOMA's new monster-free Safe Mode](https://www.pcgamer.com/frictional-on-designing-somas-new-monster-free-safe-mode/) (PC Gamer, 2017)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/5ccf670d-7102-4827-b137-8426a5379c9e
