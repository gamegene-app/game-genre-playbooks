# Should the monster follow a script or act on its own?

**Also matches:** enemy AI, AI director, stalker enemy, patrol routes, unpredictability, scripted scares

**What to weigh:** A scripted threat is reliable but learnable; one that hunts by its own senses stays unpredictable, and needs some steering so it does not ruin the pacing.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Alien: Isolation

Andy Bray and the team reasoned that a player scared all the time would give up, so they split the alien's AI in two. A director that always knows where the player is measures pressure on a menace gauge, periodically hints the alien toward them and pulls it back into the vents when the gauge peaks; the alien itself only gets a hint of direction and must find the player by its own senses.

- Source: [The Design of Alien: Isolation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wNIfQzAOKT4&t=612s) (Noclip (YouTube))
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/c7ce22a7-616a-46bb-91af-9eb6377b60cf

#### Rain World

Joar Jakobsson built Rain World's creature AI by asking how each creature could find food and get home before night, not how it could serve as an obstacle, and gave it no set patrol paths. The player gets no special treatment and gets by reading their hunting habits, hiding, tricking and outwitting them.

- Source: [Rain World: Surviving in a Living Ecosystem](https://blog.playstation.com/2017/01/16/rain-world-surviving-in-a-living-ecosystem/) (PlayStation.Blog, 2017)
- Source: [Crafting the complex, chaotic ecosystem of Rain World](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/crafting-the-complex-chaotic-ecosystem-of-i-rain-world-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2017)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/eae68e8f-f039-477f-a5a4-1b174c5d0119

## 日本語

# 怪物を台本で動かすか、自分で動かすか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 敵の AI, 監督役, 追跡者, 巡回, 予測できない, 台本の驚かし

**何を量るか:** 台本の脅威は確実だが覚えられる。自分の感覚で狩る脅威は読めないままでいられるが、テンポを壊さないよう少し舵を取る必要がある。

### この判断をした作品

#### Alien: Isolation

アンディ・ブレイらは、ずっと怖がらせ続ければ遊び手は投げ出すと考え、エイリアンのAIを2段に分けた。遊び手の位置を常に知る監督役が «脅威の目盛り» で圧を測り、時々エイリアンに方向の手がかりを与え、満ちると通気口へ退かせる。エイリアン自身は手がかりを受けるだけで、居場所は自分の感覚で突き止める。

- 出典: [The Design of Alien: Isolation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wNIfQzAOKT4&t=612s) (Noclip (YouTube))
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/c7ce22a7-616a-46bb-91af-9eb6377b60cf

#### Rain World

ヨアル・ヤコブソンは敵 AI を、遊び手を阻む障害としてでなく、餌を探して夜までに巣へ戻るという自分の目的で動く作りにし、遊び手が覚えられる決まった巡回路を持たせなかった。遊び手は特別扱いされず、相手の狩りの癖を読み、隠れ、だまし、出し抜いて進む。

- 出典: [Rain World: Surviving in a Living Ecosystem](https://blog.playstation.com/2017/01/16/rain-world-surviving-in-a-living-ecosystem/) (PlayStation.Blog, 2017)
- 出典: [Crafting the complex, chaotic ecosystem of Rain World](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/crafting-the-complex-chaotic-ecosystem-of-i-rain-world-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2017)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/eae68e8f-f039-477f-a5a4-1b174c5d0119
