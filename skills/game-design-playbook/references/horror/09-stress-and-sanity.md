# Should fear be a stat on the character, like stress or sanity?

**Also matches:** sanity meter, stress, morale, afflictions, insanity effects, recovery

**What to weigh:** A fear stat lets the game answer the player's choices with breakdowns and quirks; it also needs a way back down, and that recovery becomes a system of its own.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Darkest Dungeon

Tyler Sigman and Chris Bourassa write that the Affliction System was inspired not by Lovecraft but by human factors in history and fiction, which wargames had long handled as morale. Sigman calls it bringing a morale system into an RPG, not a new idea. Stressed heroes disobey, putting the player in a squad leader's role.

- Source: [Game Design Deep Dive: Darkest Dungeon's Affliction System](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-darkest-dungeon-s-i-affliction-system) (Gamasutra, 2015)
- Source: [Darkest Dungeon: A Design Postmortem (GDC 2016) 12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0IUaGQhlPwo&t=729s) (GDC)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/11a07648-7d70-4c93-a87b-7429edcf95e7

#### Darkest Dungeon

Heroes shed stress between missions by resting at town facilities such as the tavern and abbey. In 2015 Tyler Sigman and Chris Bourassa wrote that, having focused on the human response to stress, they also had to consider how people reduce it, and that people recuperate in different ways. Each facility has limited slots, which they say were made intentionally board-game-like.

- Source: [Game Design Deep Dive: Darkest Dungeon's Affliction System](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-darkest-dungeon-s-i-affliction-system) (Gamasutra, 2015)

## 日本語

# 怖さをストレスや正気度のような数値にするか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 正気度, ストレス, 士気, 発狂, 苦悩, 回復

**何を量るか:** 恐怖の数値があると、遊び手の選択に崩れや癖で応えられる。そのぶん下ろす手段が要り、回復そのものが1つの仕組みになる。

### この判断をした作品

#### Darkest Dungeon

シグマンとブーラサは、苦悩システムの着想はラヴクラフトではなく歴史と物語の中の人間の要素で、ウォーゲームはそれを士気として扱ってきたと書く。シグマンは、新しい考えではなく士気の仕組みを RPG へ持ち込んだのだと語る。ストレスの溜まった冒険者は命令に背き、遊び手は部隊長の立場に置かれる。

- 出典: [Game Design Deep Dive: Darkest Dungeon's Affliction System](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-darkest-dungeon-s-i-affliction-system) (Gamasutra, 2015)
- 出典: [Darkest Dungeon: A Design Postmortem (GDC 2016) 12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0IUaGQhlPwo&t=729s) (GDC)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/11a07648-7d70-4c93-a87b-7429edcf95e7

#### Darkest Dungeon

冒険者のストレスは、遠征の合間に町の酒場や修道院などの施設で休ませて下ろす。タイラー・シグマンとクリス・ブーラサは2015年、ストレスへの反応を軸にした以上は下ろし方も考えねばならず、回復の仕方も人によって違うと書く。施設ごとの枠は限られ、意図してボードゲームのように作ったという。

- 出典: [Game Design Deep Dive: Darkest Dungeon's Affliction System](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-darkest-dungeon-s-i-affliction-system) (Gamasutra, 2015)
