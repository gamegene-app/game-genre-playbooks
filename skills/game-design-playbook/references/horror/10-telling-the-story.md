# How should the story reach the player: found traces, or events happening now?

**Also matches:** environmental storytelling, audio logs, found footage, notes, narrative delivery, interface as story

**What to weigh:** Notes and recordings let the player piece together a past that cannot change; events that happen because the player is there make them a participant, but need more to be written.

### Principle: Imply rather than simulate, and let players read the meaning

Leave a gap instead of modelling or drawing everything, and let players fill it from the clues on screen. Players read patterns and intent into what they see, feel the result is their own, and the game costs less to make. The risk is that players fill the gap with the wrong thing, or that a visible break in the logic makes it look like mere code. Some designs show the numbers instead.

- First stated (2019): [Cultist Simulator: Designing an Experimental Game for Commercial Success (GDC 2019)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pBvMIUk1nQ) (Game Developers Conference, 2019)
- Restated (2019): [Designing 'Six Ages', a Storytelling Strategy Game (GDC 2019)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Xz5E7BrmCg) (Game Developers Conference, 2022)

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Firewatch

Chris Remo says Firewatch keeps some of the walking-simulator habit of piecing a past story together from journals and audio logs, but gives equal weight to a track happening now, to Henry and because of him, so the player is "a participant, not an archaeologist." Sean Vanaman says they did not want it to be like Gone Home.

- Went against this principle (`imply-dont-simulate`)
- Source: [Firewatch Interview: Into The Woods](https://gamingbolt.com/firewatch-interview-into-the-woods) (GamingBolt)
- Source: [The man behind The Walking Dead series and indie hit Firewatch reveals the secret art of telling tales](https://www.gamesradar.com/sean-vanaman-walking-dead-firewatch-interview) (GamesRadar+ (Xbox: The Official Magazine))
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/84302e91-3eeb-49e4-ab56-b2179f72a3e4

#### Her Story

Sam Barlow says that once the idea for Her Story was fully formed, he could see how its interactivity would work. He had loved text parser games in the old days, and he saw it as a way of having some of that magic without all the annoying stuff.

- Source: [Sam Barlow on mega-success and the Her Story sequel](https://www.pcgamer.com/sam-barlow-on-mega-success-and-the-her-story-sequel) (PC Gamer, 2016)

## 日本語

# 物語をどう届けるか、残された痕跡か、いま起きる出来事か

**この言葉でも当たる:** 環境で語る, 日誌, 録音, 映像の断片, 語り方, 検索

**何を量るか:** 日誌や録音は、変えられない過去を遊び手に組み立てさせる。遊び手がいるから起きる出来事は参加者にするが、書く量が増える。

### 定石: 描かずに示し、遊び手に意味を読ませる

全部を模したり描いたりせず隙間を残し、画面の手がかりから遊び手に埋めさせる。遊び手は見えたものに模様や意図を読み取り、体験を自分のものと感じる。作る費用も下がる。危険は、遊び手が別のもので隙間を埋めることと、論理の破れが見えて «ただのコード» に見えることである。数値をそのまま見せる作品もある。

- 言い出した (2019): [Cultist Simulator: Designing an Experimental Game for Commercial Success (GDC 2019)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pBvMIUk1nQ) (Game Developers Conference, 2019)
- 言い直した (2019): [Designing 'Six Ages', a Storytelling Strategy Game (GDC 2019)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Xz5E7BrmCg) (Game Developers Conference, 2022)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/joseki/imply-dont-simulate

### この判断をした作品

#### Firewatch

クリス・レモは、日誌や録音で過去の物語を組み立てさせる要素はファイアウォッチにもあるとしたうえで、いまヘンリーに起き、彼がいるせいで起きる筋を同じだけ重く置き、遊び手を «考古学者でなく参加者» にしたと語る。ショーン・ヴァナマンは Gone Home のようにはしたくなかったと言う。

- この定石の逆を選んだ (`imply-dont-simulate`)
- 出典: [Firewatch Interview: Into The Woods](https://gamingbolt.com/firewatch-interview-into-the-woods) (GamingBolt)
- 出典: [The man behind The Walking Dead series and indie hit Firewatch reveals the secret art of telling tales](https://www.gamesradar.com/sean-vanaman-walking-dead-firewatch-interview) (GamesRadar+ (Xbox: The Official Magazine))
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/84302e91-3eeb-49e4-ab56-b2179f72a3e4

#### Her Story

サム・バーロウは、Her Story の着想が頭の中で固まったとき、遊ばせ方の見通しも立ったと語る。昔のテキストパーサーのゲームを愛していた彼は、これをあの魔法の一部を厄介なところ抜きで持てるやり方だと考えた。名指ししたのはジャンルで、個々の作品ではない。

- 出典: [Sam Barlow on mega-success and the Her Story sequel](https://www.pcgamer.com/sam-barlow-on-mega-success-and-the-her-story-sequel) (PC Gamer, 2016)
