# Should the player be able to fight back?

**Also matches:** no combat, defenceless, weapons in horror, prey, power fantasy, unkillable monster

**What to weigh:** A weapon tells the player what role they have; taking it away makes them prey, but they also lose the main way to act.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Amnesia: The Dark Descent

Thomas Grip held that handing the player a tool for fighting decides what role they play. Amnesia gives no means of self-defence: monsters can only be fled from, or hidden from in darkness that eats away at sanity.

- Source: [Amnesia designer: removing combat opens up 'new horizons'](https://web.archive.org/web/2012/http://www.gamespot.com/news/amnesia-designer-removing-combat-opens-up-new-horizons-6328973) (GameSpot, 2011)
- Source: [Amnesia and the horror history (interview with Thomas Grip)](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/amnesia-horror-history-interview/) (Den of Geek, 2020)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/d236897e-7933-4fef-97ab-2e7f98187b31

#### Alien: Isolation

Recalling it in 2025, Alistair Hope said he asked his team what they would do if a tiger were loose in the office; everyone said they would hide and wait for it to leave, and he made that the core. The single alien cannot be killed: a flamethrower or a noisemaker can drive it off, but the player hides and endures as prey, not as hunter.

- Source: [Fear in unpredictability: Alien: Isolation's brand of survival horror](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/fear-in-unpredictability-i-alien-isolation-i-s-brand-of-survival-horror) (Game Developer, 2014)
- Source: [How Creative Assembly Built Its Perfectly Evolved AI Xenomorph](https://www.gameinformer.com/retrospective/2025/06/24/how-creative-assembly-built-its-perfectly-evolved-ai-xenomorph) (Game Informer, 2025)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/77aea121-adec-456f-991c-162b7b52d320

#### Clock Tower

Clock Tower's director Hifumi Kono names Alone in the Dark as his biggest inspiration, and says what he took from it was the helplessness of dying over and over without understanding the rules. Clock Tower keeps that helplessness and withholds the weapons Alone in the Dark handed the player.

- Source: [Interview: Hifumi Kono of Nude Maker and Project Scissors/NightCry](http://gaming.moe/?p=713) (Gaming.moe, 2015)
- Source: [本当に知りたい“怖さ”や“お金”の話──『クロックタワー』、『NightCry』河野一二三氏によるホラーゲーム制作のヒント解説](https://news.denfaminicogamer.jp/kikakuthetower/indiesgame_fest_mv_02) (電ファミニコゲーマー, 2016)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/a55804d7-4ec1-4303-93c2-2539602a0140

### Games that went the other way

#### Bloodborne

Masaaki Yamagiwa and Hidetaka Miyazaki say they adopted guns to change the combat from Demon's Souls and make the fighting more active; Miyazaki names its sword-and-shield combat as what they moved away from. Not wanting a shooter, they looked for an era when guns were not all-powerful, which Miyazaki calls the first reason for the Victorian setting.

- Source: [『Bloodborne』から“聖杯ダンジョン”のさらに詳しい情報が判明。TpGSで山際Pに聞く](https://dengekionline.com/elem/000/001/002/1002570/) (電撃オンライン, 2015)
- Source: [There are Guns, But From Software's Bloodborne Won't Be a Shooter](https://kotaku.com/there-are-guns-but-from-softwares-bloodborne-wont-be-a-1589789228) (Kotaku, 2014)

## 日本語

# 遊び手に反撃させるか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 戦えない, 武器なし, 反撃, 獲物, 倒せない敵, 無力さ

**何を量るか:** 武器は遊び手の役割を決めてしまう。取り上げると獲物になるが、そのぶん主な行動の手段も失う。

### この判断をした作品

#### Amnesia: The Dark Descent

トーマス・グリップは、戦う道具を渡すと、それが遊び手の役割を決めてしまうと考えた。Amnesia では身を守る手段を与えず、怪物からは逃げるか、正気を削る暗がりに隠れるしかない作りにした。

- 出典: [Amnesia designer: removing combat opens up 'new horizons'](https://web.archive.org/web/2012/http://www.gamespot.com/news/amnesia-designer-removing-combat-opens-up-new-horizons-6328973) (GameSpot, 2011)
- 出典: [Amnesia and the horror history (interview with Thomas Grip)](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/amnesia-horror-history-interview/) (Den of Geek, 2020)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/d236897e-7933-4fef-97ab-2e7f98187b31

#### Alien: Isolation

アリステア・ホープの2025年の回想では、チームに «オフィスに虎が放たれたらどうするか» と問い、誰もが隠れて立ち去るのを待つと答えたのを核に据えた。エイリアンは1体で倒せず、火炎放射器や物音で追い払うことはできても、遊び手は狩る側ではなく獲物として隠れてしのぐ。

- 出典: [Fear in unpredictability: Alien: Isolation's brand of survival horror](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/fear-in-unpredictability-i-alien-isolation-i-s-brand-of-survival-horror) (Game Developer, 2014)
- 出典: [How Creative Assembly Built Its Perfectly Evolved AI Xenomorph](https://www.gameinformer.com/retrospective/2025/06/24/how-creative-assembly-built-its-perfectly-evolved-ai-xenomorph) (Game Informer, 2025)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/77aea121-adec-456f-991c-162b7b52d320

#### Clock Tower

クロックタワーのディレクター河野一二三は、最大の着想源にアローン・イン・ザ・ダークを挙げ、規則が分からないまま死に続けた無力さを受け取ったと語る。クロックタワーはその無力さを残し、アローン・イン・ザ・ダークが持たせた武器をプレイヤーに渡さない。

- 出典: [Interview: Hifumi Kono of Nude Maker and Project Scissors/NightCry](http://gaming.moe/?p=713) (Gaming.moe, 2015)
- 出典: [本当に知りたい“怖さ”や“お金”の話──『クロックタワー』、『NightCry』河野一二三氏によるホラーゲーム制作のヒント解説](https://news.denfaminicogamer.jp/kikakuthetower/indiesgame_fest_mv_02) (電ファミニコゲーマー, 2016)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/a55804d7-4ec1-4303-93c2-2539602a0140

### 逆を選んだ作品

#### Bloodborne

山際眞晃と宮崎英高は、『デモンズソウル』から戦闘を変え、より能動的に戦わせるために銃を採り入れたと語る。宮崎は変える相手を剣と盾の戦闘と言う。ただしシューターにはしたくなかったので、銃が万能でない時代を求め、それがヴィクトリア朝を選んだ第一の理由になった (宮崎)。
