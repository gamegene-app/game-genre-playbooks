# Should the player have a companion, and how vulnerable should it be?

**Also matches:** escort, companion AI, protecting an NPC, partner, loneliness

**What to weigh:** A companion to protect raises the stakes and eases loneliness; one that dies too easily turns into a chore the player resents.

### Principle: Do not make a loss feel unfair

Avoid deaths without warning, dead ends and forced restarts, so that when the player loses, the loss feels like their own doing. Some designs break this on purpose to create tension.

- Earlier record (1988): [Classic Game Postmortem: Loom (GDC 2015)](https://archive.org/details/GDC2015Moriarty?start=1008) (GDC)
- Restated (2017): [Classic Game Postmortem: Deus Ex (GDC 2017)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tffX3VljTtI&t=1103s) (GDC)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/joseki/no-unfair-loss

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly

Makoto Shibata and Keisuke Kikuchi saw an allure in scary scenes where Mayu is attacked and the player saves her by photographing the ghost. Knowing that a Mayu who died all the time would turn players against her, they tuned Crimson Butterfly so that she does not die easily.

- Made the same call as this principle, without naming it (GameGene's reading) (`no-unfair-loss`)
- Source: [Fatal Frame's iconic camera exists to force players to "look straight at something scary" says series creator](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/survival-horror/fatal-frames-iconic-camera-exists-to-force-players-to-look-straight-at-something-scary-says-series-creator-we-thought-it-would-really-bring-out-the-scariness-of-the-ghosts/) (GamesRadar+, 2025)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/90a21188-90b2-4ecf-9b66-83ff9e15887c

#### Rule of Rose

Partway through the project, Shuji Ishikawa's team found the game was not interesting enough with Jennifer alone and, since she was so helpless and unhappy, gave her the dog Brown. Let him smell a chosen item and he leads her to the matching one, each find becoming the next scent. Yuya Takayama says his nose only reaches so far.

- Source: [Rule of Rose Staff Interview (English translation of the Hyper PlayStation 2, February 2006 issue interview, from Atlus USA's official site)](https://www.gwern.net/docs/fiction/2006-atlus.pdf) (Atlus USA (excerpt courtesy of Hyper PlayStation 2, Sony Magazines))

## 日本語

# 相棒を付けるか、どれだけ脆くするか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 護衛, 相棒, 守る相手, 同行者, 孤独

**何を量るか:** 守る相手は賭け金を上げ、孤独を和らげる。すぐ死ぬ相棒は、疎ましい手間になる。

### 定石: 理不尽に負けさせない

予告なしの死・詰み・やり直しの強制を置かず、負けたときに «自分のせい» と思えるようにする。緊張を生むために、あえてこれを破る作品もある。

- それより前の記録 (1988): [Classic Game Postmortem: Loom (GDC 2015)](https://archive.org/details/GDC2015Moriarty?start=1008) (GDC)
- 言い直した (2017): [Classic Game Postmortem: Deus Ex (GDC 2017)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tffX3VljTtI&t=1103s) (GDC)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/joseki/no-unfair-loss

### この判断をした作品

#### Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly

柴田誠と菊地啓介は、霊に襲われる繭を射影機で霊を撮って救う怖い場面に魅力を見た。ただ繭がすぐ死ぬと遊ぶ人に疎ましく思われると考え、『紅い蝶』の繭は簡単には死なないように調整した。

- 名指してはいないが、この定石と同じ判断を語っている (GameGene の読み) (`no-unfair-loss`)
- 出典: [Fatal Frame's iconic camera exists to force players to "look straight at something scary" says series creator](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/survival-horror/fatal-frames-iconic-camera-exists-to-force-players-to-look-straight-at-something-scary-says-series-creator-we-thought-it-would-really-bring-out-the-scariness-of-the-ghosts/) (GamesRadar+, 2025)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/90a21188-90b2-4ecf-9b66-83ff9e15887c

#### Rule of Rose

イシカワ・シュウジらは企画の途中で、ジェニファー1人ではゲームとして面白みが足りないと気づき、無力で不幸な彼女に相棒の犬ブラウンを付けた。選んだ品の匂いを嗅がせると、ブラウンは対応する品へ導き、見つけた品が次の手がかりになる。タカヤマ・ユウヤは、その鼻が届く距離には限りがあると語る。

- 出典: [Rule of Rose Staff Interview (English translation of the Hyper PlayStation 2, February 2006 issue interview, from Atlus USA's official site)](https://www.gwern.net/docs/fiction/2006-atlus.pdf) (Atlus USA (excerpt courtesy of Hyper PlayStation 2, Sony Magazines))
