# Which part of the familiar formula should change?

**Also matches:** differentiation, Slay the Spire clone, twist, reskin

**What to weigh:** Swapping one axis of the Slay the Spire frame (what cards fight, where they sit, how the map works) changes more than reskinning all of it.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Griftlands

Kevin Forbes says Griftlands' early conversation checks, which spent resources to sway dice rolls, were a lot less fun than fighting, and card game mechanics fit negotiating well; once deck-building came in, most of the traditional RPG systems became superfluous. PC Gamer's writer says the result swaps the dice rolls of attacks and manipulation for a hand of Slay the Spire.

- Source: [Feat of Klei: How Griftlands Exemplifies a Nurturing Approach to Game Development](https://uppercutcrit.com/feat-of-klei-how-griftlands-exemplifies-a-nurturing-approach-to-game-development/) (Uppercut)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/9c80a801-2afd-4d9e-b545-3fc5ae3b2803

#### Roguebook

Abrakam had played a lot of Slay the Spire and wanted something more than its branching path: a map to explore. The first prototype ran on the Faeria engine's board and began close to a branching path; that board grew into a hex overworld revealed with ink and brushes won in battle.

- Source: [Roguebook preview: “we want players to break the game”](https://filmstories.co.uk/gaming/roguebook-we-want-players-to-break-the-game/) (Film Stories, 2021)
- Source: [Roguebook interview: Rewriting the Slay to Spire formula](https://gamesbeat.com/roguebook-interview-rewriting-the-slay-to-spire-formula/) (GamesBeat, 2021)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/7974964a-d9ce-4267-912a-4f90b46feef4

#### Dicey Dungeons

Terry Cavanagh says Dicey Dungeons and Slay the Spire are both inspired a lot by Peter Whalen's Dream Quest. In an interview during development he said that, because the two clearly explore the same design space, he had avoided playing Slay the Spire for more than an hour or so, and looked forward to playing it properly once his game was finished.

- Source: [Dicey Dungeons: Terry Cavanagh interview](https://filmstories.co.uk/gaming/dicey-dungeons-terry-cavanagh-interview/) (Film Stories (Wireframe), 2018)
- Source: [Feature: Dicey Dungeons' Terry Cavanagh And Chipzel On Inspirations, Characters, And The Crystal Maze](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2021/01/feature_dicey_dungeons_terry_cavanagh_and_chipzel_on_inspirations_characters_and_the_crystal_maze) (Nintendo Life, 2021)

## 日本語

# 見慣れた骨格のどこを差し替えるか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 差別化, 二番煎じ, ひねり, 骨格

**何を量るか:** Slay the Spire の骨格の1つの軸 (札で何と戦うか・札をどこに置くか・地図の形) を差し替えるほうが、全部の見た目を替えるより変わる。

### この判断をした作品

#### Griftlands

ケヴィン・フォーブスによれば、資源を払ってさいころの出目を動かす初期の会話の勝負は戦闘よりずっとつまらず、交渉にはカードゲームの仕組みがよく合った。デッキ構築を加えると、伝統的な RPG の仕組みの大半は不要になった。PC Gamer の記者は、攻撃と説得のさいころを Slay the Spire の手札に置き換えたものと評する。

- 出典: [Feat of Klei: How Griftlands Exemplifies a Nurturing Approach to Game Development](https://uppercutcrit.com/feat-of-klei-how-griftlands-exemplifies-a-nurturing-approach-to-game-development/) (Uppercut)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/9c80a801-2afd-4d9e-b545-3fc5ae3b2803

#### Roguebook

アブラカムは Slay the Spire を遊び込み、その分岐する道より先へ進めて地図を探索させたいと考えた。試作は Faeria のエンジンの盤で動き、初めは分岐に近かったが、その盤が六角形の地図に育った。地図は戦闘で得たインクと筆で描き出していく。

- 出典: [Roguebook preview: “we want players to break the game”](https://filmstories.co.uk/gaming/roguebook-we-want-players-to-break-the-game/) (Film Stories, 2021)
- 出典: [Roguebook interview: Rewriting the Slay to Spire formula](https://gamesbeat.com/roguebook-interview-rewriting-the-slay-to-spire-formula/) (GamesBeat, 2021)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/7974964a-d9ce-4267-912a-4f90b46feef4

#### Dicey Dungeons

テリー・カヴァナーは、本作と Slay the Spire はどちらもピーター・ウェイレンのドリームクエストに強く着想を得たと語る。開発中の取材では、同じ設計の領域を探っているのが明らかなので Slay the Spire は1時間ほどより長く遊ばずにきた、本作が完成したらきちんと遊びたいと話した。

- 出典: [Dicey Dungeons: Terry Cavanagh interview](https://filmstories.co.uk/gaming/dicey-dungeons-terry-cavanagh-interview/) (Film Stories (Wireframe), 2018)
- 出典: [Feature: Dicey Dungeons' Terry Cavanagh And Chipzel On Inspirations, Characters, And The Crystal Maze](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2021/01/feature_dicey_dungeons_terry_cavanagh_and_chipzel_on_inspirations_characters_and_the_crystal_maze) (Nintendo Life, 2021)
