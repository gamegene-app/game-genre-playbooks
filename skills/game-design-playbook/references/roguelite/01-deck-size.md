# How thick should the deck be, and how easy should removing cards be?

**Also matches:** deck thinning, card removal, deck bloat, trimming the deck

**What to weigh:** A thin deck makes each pick matter and lets combos fire reliably; cheap removal makes thinning the dominant strategy.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Roguebook

Richard Garfield called it ridiculous how much deckbuilders are about removing cards rather than adding them; he likes narrow decks too, but not every game should work that way. Roguebook grants a passive power each time the deck crosses a size threshold, giving players a reason to build a big, varied deck.

- Source: [Roguebook preview: “we want players to break the game”](https://filmstories.co.uk/gaming/roguebook-we-want-players-to-break-the-game/) (Film Stories, 2021)
- Source: [Roguebook interview: Rewriting the Slay to Spire formula](https://gamesbeat.com/roguebook-interview-rewriting-the-slay-to-spire-formula/) (GamesBeat, 2021)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/18ac43cf-cc51-493e-a8f2-93eef1b44056

#### Luck be a Landlord

In a March 2021 early access patch, Dan DiIorio added a currency for removing symbols from the inventory, alongside one for adding symbols. He raised the rent because the new currencies made the game easier, and cut the Ninja and Mouse multiplier because removal made that build easier to reach. He kept retuning the Removal Token supply; he himself enjoyed higher rent with fewer removal tokens, but moved that difficulty to the apartment floors rather than force it on every player.

- Source: [Luck be a Landlord: Content Patch #5 -- Apartment Floors](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1404850/view/4023377846279336065) (Steam (TrampolineTales), 2021)
- Source: [Luck be a Landlord: Content Patch #4 -- More Languages](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1404850/view/4048146376714610834) (Steam (TrampolineTales), 2021)

## 日本語

# デッキの厚さと、札を取り除く手段をどこまで用意するか

**この言葉でも当たる:** デッキ圧縮, 札の除去, デッキが膨らむ, 薄いデッキ

**何を量るか:** 薄いデッキは1枚の選択を重くし、コンボを確実に回す。取り除く手段が安いと、薄くすることが最善の一手になる。

### この判断をした作品

#### Roguebook

リチャード・ガーフィールドは、デッキ構築ゲームが札を足すより削ることばかりなのはばかげていると言い、絞ったデッキも好きだが、すべてのゲームがそうあるべきではないと語る。Roguebook は枚数が節目を越えるたびに受け身の能力を与え、大きく雑多なデッキを組む理由を作った。

- 出典: [Roguebook preview: “we want players to break the game”](https://filmstories.co.uk/gaming/roguebook-we-want-players-to-break-the-game/) (Film Stories, 2021)
- 出典: [Roguebook interview: Rewriting the Slay to Spire formula](https://gamesbeat.com/roguebook-interview-rewriting-the-slay-to-spire-formula/) (GamesBeat, 2021)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/18ac43cf-cc51-493e-a8f2-93eef1b44056

#### Luck be a Landlord

ダン・ディオリオは早期アクセス中の2021年3月、絵柄を外せる通貨を、絵柄を足す通貨と共に足した。新しい通貨で易しくなったとして家賃を上げ、除去で作りやすくなった Ninja and Mouse の倍率を下げた。その後も除去トークンを配る数を調整し、高い家賃と少ない除去トークンは本人も好んだが、全員には課さずアパートの階層へ移した。

- 出典: [Luck be a Landlord: Content Patch #5 -- Apartment Floors](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1404850/view/4023377846279336065) (Steam (TrampolineTales), 2021)
- 出典: [Luck be a Landlord: Content Patch #4 -- More Languages](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1404850/view/4048146376714610834) (Steam (TrampolineTales), 2021)
