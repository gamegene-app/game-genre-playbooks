# Should the game explain its rules, or let the player discover them?

**Also matches:** tutorial, onboarding, teaching without words, hints, manual, first-time user experience

**What to weigh:** Discovery makes understanding the player's own and keeps surprises intact; with no guidance at all, playtesters get lost, so something small usually has to point the way.

### Principle: Teach through play

Teach the rules through the order in which the player meets things, one idea at a time, instead of through text or a separate tutorial. Some games teach nothing and leave the player to find their own way, or hide things for players to share.

- Restated (2015): [Miyamoto on World 1-1: How Nintendo made Mario's most iconic level](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRGRJRUWafY) (Eurogamer)
- Restated (2017): [The Witness Documentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YdSdvIRkkDY) (Noclip, 2017)

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### The Witness

Jonathan Blow wanted a clear slate for communication without words, so he removed jumping and picking things up. Instead of a tutorial in words, puzzles lined up from easy panels convey each rule.

- Source: ['The Witness' Creator: Jonathan Blow Interview](https://time.com/4355763/the-witness-jonathan-blow-interview) (TIME, 2016)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/bd76ce64-aebd-44df-b85f-0a37bd72ae66

#### Strange Horticulture

The Donkin brothers saw that much of a board game's excitement comes before the box is even opened, and that opening it reveals pieces whose uses are a mystery. To recreate that discovery they left out the tutorial and added items of unclear use, such as an etched disc, balanced by animations that draw the eye.

- Source: [Deep Dive: How the Strange Horticulture devs set out to make your next favorite game](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/deep-dive-strange-horticulture) (Game Developer, 2022)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/454a1ae1-049b-4acd-ba74-de8f663c10b0

#### TUNIC

Shouldice recalls that the first Legend of Zelda had things you could only learn from notes in its manual's margins, and his first drafts of TUNIC's manual emulated that booklet before he reworked it. TUNIC hides hints and techniques in manual pages scattered through the world, since a full-screen tutorial popup can ruin any sense of wonder.

- Source: [Tunic Interview: Andrew Shouldice Discusses Iterative Development, Inspirations, Zelda Comparisons, and More](https://gamerant.com/tunic-interview-andrew-shouldice-development-journey-zelda-inspirations/) (Game Rant, 2022)
- Source: [How TUNIC weaves wondrous worlds inspired by inscrutable NES manuals](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/road-to-igf-2023/how-tunic-weaves-wondrous-unknowable-worlds-inspired-by-inscrutable-nes-manuals) (Game Developer, 2023)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/20b5d94c-03a5-409d-b70e-3c89921a5149

#### Rain World (horror)

James Therrien and Videocult first aimed for no guidance or tutorial, so that a mechanic found by accident would open other branches, which he called the opposite of the metroidvania where keys open regions. Testing showed this did not work, so as a compromise the first screens give short text, and a small holographic creature guides the player toward food and safety and warns of dangers.

- Source: [Crafting the complex, chaotic ecosystem of Rain World](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/crafting-the-complex-chaotic-ecosystem-of-i-rain-world-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2017)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/b2f513a8-e760-4650-bc22-3c708da3fa71

## 日本語

# 規則を説明するか、遊び手に見つけさせるか

**この言葉でも当たる:** チュートリアル, 導入, 言葉を使わずに教える, ヒント, 説明書, 最初の体験

**何を量るか:** 見つけさせると、分かったことが遊び手自身のものになり、驚きも壊れない。何の導きも無いと試遊で迷う人が出るので、たいてい小さな道しるべが要る。

### 定石: 遊ばせて教える

決まりを文章や別立てのチュートリアルで説明せず、出会う順番で1つずつ遊ばせて覚えさせる。何も教えずに遊び手を迷わせる作品や、わざと伏せて遊び手どうしで教え合わせる作品もある。

- 言い直した (2015): [Miyamoto on World 1-1: How Nintendo made Mario's most iconic level](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRGRJRUWafY) (Eurogamer)
- 言い直した (2017): [The Witness Documentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YdSdvIRkkDY) (Noclip, 2017)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/joseki/teach-through-play

### この判断をした作品

#### The Witness

ジョナサン・ブロウは、言葉を使わない伝達のための白紙が欲しくて、跳ぶことも物を拾うこともできなくした。言葉で説明するチュートリアルは置かず、易しい盤から並べた謎そのものに規則を伝えさせた。

- 出典: ['The Witness' Creator: Jonathan Blow Interview](https://time.com/4355763/the-witness-jonathan-blow-interview) (TIME, 2016)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/bd76ce64-aebd-44df-b85f-0a37bd72ae66

#### Strange Horticulture

ドンキン兄弟は、ボードゲームの興奮の多くは箱を開ける前から始まり、開ければ用途の謎めいた部品が見つかると気づいた。その発見の感覚を写すためチュートリアルを置かず、記号を刻んだ円盤のような用途の分からない道具を足した。戸惑わないよう目を引くアニメで釣り合いを取った。

- 出典: [Deep Dive: How the Strange Horticulture devs set out to make your next favorite game](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/deep-dive-strange-horticulture) (Game Developer, 2022)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/454a1ae1-049b-4acd-ba74-de8f663c10b0

#### TUNIC

ショールダイスは、初代『ゼルダの伝説』には説明書の余白の書き込みでしか分からないことがあったと振り返り、TUNIC の説明書の最初の試作もその冊子を真似た (後に作り直した)。TUNIC はヒントや技を説明書のページに書き、世界に散らして隠した。全画面のチュートリアルは驚きを壊しうるからである。

- 出典: [Tunic Interview: Andrew Shouldice Discusses Iterative Development, Inspirations, Zelda Comparisons, and More](https://gamerant.com/tunic-interview-andrew-shouldice-development-journey-zelda-inspirations/) (Game Rant, 2022)
- 出典: [How TUNIC weaves wondrous worlds inspired by inscrutable NES manuals](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/road-to-igf-2023/how-tunic-weaves-wondrous-unknowable-worlds-inspired-by-inscrutable-nes-manuals) (Game Developer, 2023)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/20b5d94c-03a5-409d-b70e-3c89921a5149

#### Rain World (horror)

ジェームズ・テリエンらは当初、導きもチュートリアルも置かず、偶然見つけた操作で別の道が開ける作りを狙った。本人はこれを «鍵で地域を開くメトロイドヴァニアの逆» と呼ぶ。試遊で導きなしでは通じないと分かり、妥協として最初の画面に短い文を出し、餌と安全へ導いて危険を知らせる小さなホログラムの生き物を置いた。

- 出典: [Crafting the complex, chaotic ecosystem of Rain World](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/crafting-the-complex-chaotic-ecosystem-of-i-rain-world-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2017)
