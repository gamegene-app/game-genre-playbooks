# How should card rewards be offered, and can the player turn them down?

**What to weigh:** Showing rewards early lets players plan; a pick that is always correct stops being a choice.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Vault of the Void

Josh Bruce felt that too much randomness makes losses feel unfair, so, departing from typical roguelike deckbuilders, he showed events, the map and reward cards in advance, wanting a loss to feel like the player's own fault. The developer also lets any card be Purged into energy, and reviewers read this as refining the Slay the Spire formula.

- Source: [Cleanse The Corruption in Roguelike Deckbuilder Vault of the Void Releasing For PC on 4th October](https://gamespress.com/Cleanse-The-Corruption-in-Roguelike-Deckbuilder-Vault-of-the-Void-Rele) (Games Press, 2022)
- Source: [開発者&翻訳者「本当に翻訳に必要なのはただ情熱」デッキ構築ローグライト『Vault of the Void』インタビュー【有志日本語化の現場から】](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2022/04/10/117645.html) (Game*Spark, 2022)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/37acbc87-d53c-4200-aee4-2414e9df4452

#### Monster Train

Mark Cooke names as a design goal the moment when the one piece you were hoping for completes a drafted build and you shout "Yes!". Yet a spell that grants multistrike was nerfed over and over, its Ember cost raised and a Fragile effect added. Nerfs are sometimes needed, he says, because picking the same thing every time is not interesting.

- Source: [Shiny Shoe's CEO Talks Monster Train, Inkbound, & Creating "Poggable Moments" For Players](https://screenrant.com/inkbound-monster-train-mark-cooke-interview/) (Screen Rant, 2024)

## 日本語

# 報酬の札をどう見せ、断れるようにするか

**何を量るか:** 報酬を先に見せると計画が立つ。毎回取るのが正解になる札は、選択でなくなる。

### この判断をした作品

#### Vault of the Void

ジョシュ・ブルースは、運が強すぎると負けが不公平に感じられると考え、一般的なデッキ構築ローグライトと違う手を取った。イベント・マップ・報酬の札をすべて先に見せ、負けを自分のせいと感じさせたかったという。開発元はどの札もエネルギーに換えられる作りも挙げ、評者はこれを Slay the Spire の型を磨いたものと見る。

- 出典: [Cleanse The Corruption in Roguelike Deckbuilder Vault of the Void Releasing For PC on 4th October](https://gamespress.com/Cleanse-The-Corruption-in-Roguelike-Deckbuilder-Vault-of-the-Void-Rele) (Games Press, 2022)
- 出典: [開発者&翻訳者「本当に翻訳に必要なのはただ情熱」デッキ構築ローグライト『Vault of the Void』インタビュー【有志日本語化の現場から】](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2022/04/10/117645.html) (Game*Spark, 2022)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/37acbc87-d53c-4200-aee4-2414e9df4452

#### Monster Train

マーク・クックは、一回の旅で拾った札や品が噛み合い、«これさえ来れば完璧» と待った1枚が揃って声が出る瞬間を作ることを設計の目標に挙げる。一方、多段攻撃を与える呪文の札は強すぎて何度も弱め、エンバーのコストを上げ、脆弱 (Fragile) を付ける効果も足した。弱体化は時に要る、毎回同じものを取るのが最善では面白くないからだという。

- 出典: [Shiny Shoe's CEO Talks Monster Train, Inkbound, & Creating "Poggable Moments" For Players](https://screenrant.com/inkbound-monster-train-mark-cooke-interview/) (Screen Rant, 2024)
