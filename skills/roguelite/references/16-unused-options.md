# What to do with cards and options that almost nobody uses?

**Also matches:** dead cards, situational cards, conditional cards, narrow cards, nobody picks it, pick rate, unused content

**What to weigh:** An option nobody picks is wasted content; giving it a payoff, changing its shape or moving it earlier can bring it back.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Rogue Legacy 2

Teddy Lee looked back that in the first game, traits were first-time surprises that players stopped picking once they were no longer curious. For Rogue Legacy 2 he set out to "make everything have a trade-off": the more a trait hurts, the more gold the run earns, to keep players testing themselves and to ease the habit of dying at once to reroll heirs.

- Source: [How Rogue Legacy 2 aims to go from a castle to a full kingdom](https://shacknews.com/article/117448/how-rogue-legacy-2-aims-to-go-from-a-castle-to-a-full-kingdom) (Shacknews, 2020)
- Source: [Indie World Interview: Rogue Legacy 2 game designer Teddy Lee talks about the newly available heir](https://www.nintendo.com/whatsnew/indie-world-interview-rogue-legacy-2-game-designer-teddy-lee-talks-about-the-newly-available-heir/) (Nintendo of America, 2022)

#### Rogue Legacy 2

A Cellar Door developer likened relics to the power-up Hades gives for clearing each floor, but said that in a game that is not arena-based, as most roguelikes are, players would feel obliged to clear every biome for relics. Relics first took a percentage of max health and nobody picked them up, so the team built Resolve as a balance that leaves players who skip relics a chance at the boss.

- Source: [Rogue Legacy 2 Lead Designer Discusses 1.0 Release, Classes, and More](https://gamerant.com/rogue-legacy-2-release-update-classes-platforms-interview/) (Game Rant, 2022)
- Source: [The Making of Rogue Legacy 2 | Noclip Documentary (20:15)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3aFy8F0aRw&t=1215s) (Noclip)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/2f45cd76-fd86-4759-9b50-d6f77156670e

#### Monster Train 2

Mark Cooke called it a design mistake that Monster Train's Expert Challenges, runs with fixed starting conditions, opened only after a clear at the highest difficulty, so many players never saw them. Monster Train 2 moved them to the Dimensional Portal, which unlocks much earlier.

- Source: [Shiny Shoe CEO Mark Cooke explains why Monster Train needed a sequel instead of more DLC](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/shiny-shoe-ceo-mark-cooke-explains-why-monster-train-needed-a-seq/zd8975) (Gamepressure, 2025)

## 日本語

# ほとんど使われない札や選択肢をどうするか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 死に札, 条件つきの札, 狭い札, 使われない, 選ばれない, 採用率

**何を量るか:** 誰も選ばない選択肢は作った手間が無駄になる。見返りを付ける、形を変える、早く出す、で戻せることがある。

### この判断をした作品

#### Rogue Legacy 2

テディ・リーは、前作の特性は初回の驚きにとどまり、物珍しさが消えると選ばれなくなったと振り返る。本作では «すべてに代償と見返りを» と考え、不利な特性ほど周回で多くの金貨が入るようにした。遊び手に難しい後継ぎを試させ、城に入ってすぐ自滅して後継ぎを引き直す遊び方も和らげるためだという。

- 出典: [How Rogue Legacy 2 aims to go from a castle to a full kingdom](https://shacknews.com/article/117448/how-rogue-legacy-2-aims-to-go-from-a-castle-to-a-full-kingdom) (Shacknews, 2020)
- 出典: [Indie World Interview: Rogue Legacy 2 game designer Teddy Lee talks about the newly available heir](https://www.nintendo.com/whatsnew/indie-world-interview-rogue-legacy-2-game-designer-teddy-lee-talks-about-the-newly-available-heir/) (Nintendo of America, 2022)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/e513ccf2-e774-414b-a5eb-4de38eaafb5c

#### Rogue Legacy 2

セラードア・ゲームズの開発者は遺物を『ハデス』で階を抜けるたびに得る強化になぞらえつつ、多くのローグライクと違い闘技場型でない本作では、遊び手が遺物のために全部の場所を回らされると考えた。最初は最大体力を割合で削る形で誰も拾わず、集めない人にもボスに勝つ目を残す釣り合いとしてリゾルブを作った。

- 出典: [Rogue Legacy 2 Lead Designer Discusses 1.0 Release, Classes, and More](https://gamerant.com/rogue-legacy-2-release-update-classes-platforms-interview/) (Game Rant, 2022)
- 出典: [The Making of Rogue Legacy 2 | Noclip Documentary (20:15)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3aFy8F0aRw&t=1215s) (Noclip)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/2f45cd76-fd86-4759-9b50-d6f77156670e

#### Monster Train 2

マーク・クックは、前作『モンスタートレイン』で開始条件の決まった «エキスパートチャレンジ» が最高難度を越えるまで開かず、多くの遊び手が触れずに終わったことを設計の誤りと見た。本作はそれを早く開く «次元ポータル» へ移し、より多くの人が遊べるようにした。

- 出典: [Shiny Shoe CEO Mark Cooke explains why Monster Train needed a sequel instead of more DLC](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/shiny-shoe-ceo-mark-cooke-explains-why-monster-train-needed-a-seq/zd8975) (Gamepressure, 2025)
