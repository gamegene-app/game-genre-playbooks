# How long should one run be?

**Also matches:** session length, run time, pacing, short runs

**What to weigh:** A long run raises the stakes of each death; letting players pick the length can serve both sides.

### Principle: Value the player's time

Cut repetition that only costs time, such as walking back for lost items, replaying long stretches, grinding, or reloading until the odds go your way, or give the repetition a new meaning. Some games take away what the player built on purpose to give it weight.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Into the Breach

Matthew Davis felt FTL ran longer than expected and asking for two or three hours was not great. At GDC 2019 he adds that the upgrade curve gave no single length: he liked two islands, Justin Ma four. Ma proposed choosing when to do the final mission, and they let the game end any time after two islands.

- Source: [How Subset Games made the jump from FTL to Into the Breach](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-subset-games-made-the-jump-from-i-ftl-i-to-i-into-the-breach-i-) (Gamasutra, 2018)
- Source: [Into the Breach Design Postmortem (GDC 2019), 43:00-45:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s_I07Iq_2XM&t=2580s) (GDC (YouTube), 2019)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/8c4a36cb-5113-4c91-a984-dedfeb3be23d

#### Book of Demons

Thing Trunk's Maciej Biedrzycki and his colleagues, now adults with jobs and families, no longer had the play time they had as children. So they built Flexiscope: the player first picks how many minutes to play, and the game generates dungeon levels meant to play like a complete quest of about that length, with fights, treasure and a boss.

- Source: [A closer look at the deck-building hack-and-slash success, Book of Demons](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-closer-look-at-the-deck-building-hack-and-slash-success-i-book-of-demons-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2019)
- Source: [Interview: Book of Demons Mixes Diablo with Paper Mario](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/263560/interview-book-of-demons-mixes-diablo-with-paper-mario/) (VGChartz, 2016)

## 日本語

# 1回の run の長さをどう決めるか

**この言葉でも当たる:** 1回の長さ, プレイ時間, テンポ, 短いラン

**何を量るか:** 長い run は1回の死を重くする。長さを遊び手に選ばせると、両方に応えられることがある。

### 定石: 遊び手の時間を無駄にさせない

落とし物を取りに戻る・長い区間のやり直し・レベル上げ・確率に勝つまでのロードのように、時間だけを取る繰り返しを削るか、繰り返しに別の意味を持たせる。遊び手の積み上げをあえて奪い、重みを出す作品もある。

- 言い直した (2010): [Keynote: Everything You Know Is Wrong (GDC 2010)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bY7aRJE-oOY) (GDC)
- 言い直した (2019): ["King Me": A Defense of King-Making in Board Game Design (GDC 2019 Board Game Design Day)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UraJElx1ebg) (Game Developers Conference, 2021)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/joseki/value-the-players-time

### この判断をした作品

#### Into the Breach

マシュー・デイヴィスは、FTL が思ったより長くなり2〜3時間を約束させたのはよくなかったと振り返る。GDC 2019 では、強化の伸びから長さが1つに決まらず、自分は島2つ、ジャスティン・マは島4つを好んだとも語る。マの案で最終任務へ向かう時を選べるようにし、島2つの後はいつでも終えられる形にした。

- 出典: [How Subset Games made the jump from FTL to Into the Breach](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-subset-games-made-the-jump-from-i-ftl-i-to-i-into-the-breach-i-) (Gamasutra, 2018)
- 出典: [Into the Breach Design Postmortem (GDC 2019), 43:00-45:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s_I07Iq_2XM&t=2580s) (GDC (YouTube), 2019)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/8c4a36cb-5113-4c91-a984-dedfeb3be23d

#### Book of Demons

Thing Trunk のマチェイ・ビェドジツキらは、大人になって仕事や家族を抱え、子どもの頃ほど遊ぶ時間が取れなくなった。そこで遊び手が先に何分遊ぶかを選ぶと、おおよそその長さで遊び終える、戦い・宝探し・ボスまで揃った冒険のような階の連なりを生成する «Flexiscope» を作った。

- 出典: [A closer look at the deck-building hack-and-slash success, Book of Demons](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-closer-look-at-the-deck-building-hack-and-slash-success-i-book-of-demons-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2019)
- 出典: [Interview: Book of Demons Mixes Diablo with Paper Mario](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/263560/interview-book-of-demons-mixes-diablo-with-paper-mario/) (VGChartz, 2016)
