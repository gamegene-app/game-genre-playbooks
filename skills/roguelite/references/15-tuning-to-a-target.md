# What clear rate should the game aim for, and how do you tune toward it?

**Also matches:** win rate, clear rate, balance data, playtest data, bots, autoplay, telemetry, balance patches

**What to weigh:** A low clear rate can be the point of a skill game; tuning toward a target needs data, and players react to how it feels more than to the number.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Slay the Spire

Asked in 2020 how to keep players motivated through roguelike losses, Anthony Giovannetti said Mega Crit was not particularly worried: the many deck permutations made replayability high even in early playtests, and the key was letting players quickly try new strategies. In 2018 he called the low win rate intentional, to keep the game skill-based.

- Source: [Road to the IGF: Mega Crit Games' Slay the Spire](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-mega-crit-games-i-slay-the-spire-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2020)
- Source: [Slay the Spire designer discusses new characters and card game inspirations](https://www.pcgamer.com/slay-the-spire-designer-discusses-new-characters-and-card-game-inspirations/) (PC Gamer, 2018)

## 日本語

# クリア率をどこに置き、どう合わせるか

**この言葉でも当たる:** クリア率, 勝率, 調整, 計測, ボット, 自動プレイ, 試遊のデータ, バランス調整

**何を量るか:** 腕で勝つゲームなら、低いクリア率そのものが狙いになりうる。目標に合わせるにはデータが要り、遊び手は数字より手触りに反応する。

### この判断をした作品

#### Slay the Spire

ローグライクの負けで遊び手の気持ちが折れないかと2020年に問われたアンソニー・ジョヴァネッティは、特に心配しなかったと答えた。組めるデッキの幅で試遊の初めから繰り返し遊ばれ、大事なのは新しい戦い方をすぐ試させることだと言う。2018年には、勝率の低さは腕で勝つゲームにする意図だと語った。

- 出典: [Road to the IGF: Mega Crit Games' Slay the Spire](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-mega-crit-games-i-slay-the-spire-i-) (Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2020)
- 出典: [Slay the Spire designer discusses new characters and card game inspirations](https://www.pcgamer.com/slay-the-spire-designer-discusses-new-characters-and-card-game-inspirations/) (PC Gamer, 2018)
