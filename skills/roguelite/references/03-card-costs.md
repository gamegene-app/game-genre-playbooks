# How does the player pay for cards?

**What to weigh:** The cost system decides whether a turn is a puzzle over a hand or just playing everything you drew.

### Shipped games that faced this call

#### Slay the Spire

Anthony Giovannetti, who played Magic and Netrunner growing up, wanted a cost system for cards: rather than playing out the whole hand as in many deckbuilders, he gave Slay the Spire an energy system "like mana" to make individual combat decisions more interesting.

- Source: [Slay the Spire designer discusses new characters and card game inspirations](https://www.pcgamer.com/slay-the-spire-designer-discusses-new-characters-and-card-game-inspirations/) (PC Gamer, 2018)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/a0b01bf5-4bf3-4ccb-9085-081554f886d2

#### Black Book

Vladimir Beletsky felt that card combat made more sense when a single hero carries the story, looked at popular roguelikes on Steam and took Slay the Spire as his starting point. So as not to copy its mechanics, he replaced mana with slots for words, so that in battle the player builds folk incantations (zagovory) whose words all affect each other.

- Source: [Magic, devils and mythology of northern Russia - an interview about dark RPG Black Book](https://enklawanetwork.pl/pc/podlupa/1938-magic-devils-and-mythology-of-northern-russia-an-interview-about-dark-rpg-black-book/) (EnklawaNetwork.pl, 2020)
- Source: [Интервью с Владимиром Белецким — создателем игры Black Book](https://skillbox.ru/media/gamedev/interview-vladimir-beletskiy/) (Skillbox Media, 2021)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/en/library/records/claims/624120a4-efc7-47cd-b4cd-5a0e2e8aa065

#### One Turn Kill

DenDen made the precursor at a university club hackathon in winter 2021. In the released game, the cost of playing a card is drawing from the deck, so every play refills the hand, but a card cannot be played when too few cards remain. Paired with having to win in one turn, the draw is meant to feel exhilarating while one mistake can be fatal.

- Source: [「敵にターンを回すと負け」ワンターン必殺カードバトル『One Turn Kill』来年1月16日Steamでリリースへ。やるかやられるかの1ターンバトル](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20251012-361416/) (AUTOMATON, 2025)

## 日本語

# 札のコストを何で払わせるか

**何を量るか:** コストの仕組みで、1手番が手札の組み立てになるか、引いた札を全部出すだけになるかが決まる。

### この判断をした作品

#### Slay the Spire

マジックやネットランナーを遊んで育ったアンソニー・ジョヴァネッティは、札にコストの仕組みを置きたかった。多くのデッキ構築ゲームのように手札を出し切るのではなく、«マナのような» エネルギーを置き、戦闘の一手ごとの判断を面白くしようとした。

- 出典: [Slay the Spire designer discusses new characters and card game inspirations](https://www.pcgamer.com/slay-the-spire-designer-discusses-new-characters-and-card-game-inspirations/) (PC Gamer, 2018)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/a0b01bf5-4bf3-4ccb-9085-081554f886d2

#### Black Book

ウラジーミル・ベレツキーは、主人公1人が物語を担うなら札の戦闘のほうが筋が通ると考え、Steam で人気のローグライクを見て Slay the Spire を出発点にした。その仕組みを写さないようマナを語の枠に替え、戦闘で民間の呪文 (ザゴヴォル) を組み、語どうしが効き合う形にした。

- 出典: [Magic, devils and mythology of northern Russia - an interview about dark RPG Black Book](https://enklawanetwork.pl/pc/podlupa/1938-magic-devils-and-mythology-of-northern-russia-an-interview-about-dark-rpg-black-book/) (EnklawaNetwork.pl, 2020)
- 出典: [Интервью с Владимиром Белецким — создателем игры Black Book](https://skillbox.ru/media/gamedev/interview-vladimir-beletskiy/) (Skillbox Media, 2021)
- GameGene: https://gamegene.app/library/records/claims/624120a4-efc7-47cd-b4cd-5a0e2e8aa065

#### One Turn Kill

前身版は電電が2021年度冬季の大学サークルのハッカソンで作った。製品版ではカードを使うコストが山札からのドローで、使うたびに手札は補われるが、山札が足りなければカードは使えない。1ターンで倒しきる制約と組み合わせ、引く爽快感と一手のミスが命取りになる緊張を狙った。
